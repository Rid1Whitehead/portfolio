Post-Doctoral Researcher · LET Lab, University of Oulu

I study how gaze, posture and proximity shape collaborative learning.

I build computer vision and multimodal LLM methods that turn classroom video into evidence about how groups regulate their learning together. All of my methods are open source. I am part of the Learning, Education and Technology Research Lab (LET Lab) at the University of Oulu, Finland.

Projects

Gaze analysis from 2D video

A workflow for analysing gaze behaviour in groups using only ordinary front-facing 2D video, with no eye trackers required.

Paper: JCAL 2024 Paper: JLA 2025 Computer vision

Proxemics analysis from 2D video

A novel workflow for measuring how close group members sit and move relative to each other. Used in the LEAD project to study proxemics in collaborative learning.

Paper: IJCSCL 2026 Computer vision

MLLM-based posture analysis

Large-scale posture analysis of collaborative learning video with multimodal large language models, plus a dashboard for running custom analyses.

Paper: JLA 2025 Code Dashboard

Multimodal data collection dashboard

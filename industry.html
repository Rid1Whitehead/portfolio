AI should support learners' thinking, not do it for them.

Generative AI makes it easy to hand over the thinking that makes learning stick: planning, monitoring and reflecting. This is called metacognitive laziness. Learning takes effort, and that effort is where learning happens. With company partners, I work on AI that collaborates with learners: it prompts and supports their self-regulated learning, while the planning, monitoring, reflecting and productive struggle stay with the learner.

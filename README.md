# Ridwan Whitehead | Research Portfolio

Personal website of **Ridwan Whitehead**, Postdoctoral Researcher at the Learning, Education and Technology Research Lab (LET Lab), University of Oulu, Finland.

**Visit the site: https://rid1whitehead.github.io/portfolio/**

My work has two sides:

- **Research:** computer vision and multimodal LLM methods for studying nonverbal behaviour (gaze, posture, proxemics) in collaborative learning and socially shared regulation of learning.
- **Industry collaboration:** a Business Finland co-creation project with company partners on metacognitive laziness, and how AI can support learners' self-regulation instead of replacing it.

The site has a landing page plus separate pages for research (with an interactive gaze, posture and proximity demo), industry work, other tools, and publications (loaded from Semantic Scholar).
